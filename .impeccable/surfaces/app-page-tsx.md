# Surface brief: FORM / 27 store (home, shop, product, lookbook, campaign, about, bag, size guide)

Scope: every route on branch redesign/axis-plan. Visitor mode: Persuade (home, editorial) with Operate duties (shop, product, bag). The audience to impress first is portfolio reviewers, confirmed by the owner, and the shopping flow must stay credible and fast.

Audience: prospective clients judging Alson's craft, plus the fictional design persona of style-aware shoppers. Action: reach a piece, choose a size, add to bag. Proof: the eight garments in five views and four campaign photographs. Constraints: concept and AI-imagery disclosures stay; no fake commerce claims. Owner keeps: citron (#d7f247), the FORM / 27 name, OFF / AXIS, product copy and all imagery.

Memorable moment: the hero plan, where the photograph is knocked off the building grid by a citron axis line that follows the visitor's pointer and reads out its angle live.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Axis Plan. The store is an architect's plan of the concrete building where OFF / AXIS was shot. Every garment rests rotated off the structural grid and snaps onto its axis when touched. This refuses the fashion category's full-bleed hero, product-card grid and ticker.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is drafting paper (#ecebe6) with board-marked concrete fields (#d3d2cc) and solid black section-cut bands (#121212). Hairline ink grid with circled axis bubbles A–H across and 1–4 down. Dimension strings and leader lines in Martian Mono. Display set in Mona Sans, from wide to condensed. The single citron off-axis line is the only colour and marks every active state. No radii and no cards; elevation comes from section-cut shadows only.

STORY: The visitor sees the collection as a building plan, turns and inspects garments as elevations, chooses a size by reading its dimensions, and adds to a bag set out as a bill of quantities.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport plan grid with axis bubbles on the top and left edges. The hero photograph is a large plate rotated −4° that sits across axes C–H. The citron axis line crosses the whole sheet through the plate at the same angle, and a live readout labels it "AXIS −4.0°". OFF / AXIS is set at architectural scale across axes A–E, stretched wide. "Shop the collection" is a citron block on axis 3. The drawing title block runs along the bottom edge.

FORM: Architect's plan and section drawing (the owner's pick, ranked first of 7 on my ordered list; the roll assigned position 3); seed key 169df205. Raises:
- Every composition holds one asymmetric triangle of plate, type and charged empty grid (from the ikebana challenger).
- Citron is reserved for what is active now (glazier).
- Choosing a size isolates its dimension string and ghosts the others (detector).
- The five views swap in place inside one fixed sheet frame (ASCII render).
- Idle pieces sit off-axis and register on interaction (comic misregistration).
- The loudest word is the door, and entrances land in one beat (street poster, competitive).

Signature interactions:
- The pointer steers the off-axis angle with a spring.
- Scrolling travels along the collection axis.
- Drag to turn a garment front to back.
- A loupe for inspecting fabric.
- A size picker that rewrites the dimension strings.
- Add-to-bag flies the piece into the title block.
- Headline width stretches with scroll velocity.
- A motion toggle lets visitors whose system reduces motion opt in, and a reduced path keeps fades and state feedback.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
